## ☕ Coffee's For You

A cozy, premium coffee blog and shop website built for coffee enthusiasts who appreciate the art behind every brew. The site shares brewing tips, recipes, coffee stories, and a shop — all wrapped in a warm, dark-roast-inspired design.

Built with WordPress.

---

## 📖 About

Welcome to **The Coffee** — a digital sanctuary crafted for those who believe coffee is more than a morning caffeine fix. It's a daily ritual, a source of comfort, and a way of life.

The site covers everything from traditional dark roast flavors and expert brewing techniques to exclusive recipes readers can recreate at home, delivered through blog posts, a photo gallery, and a shop.

![About Section](images/About.png)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Home / Hero** — Eye-catching landing section with a newsletter sign-up
- **About** — The story and mission behind the blog
- **Blog** — Coffee stories, brewing tips, and recipes with a latest posts feed
- **Gallery** — A curated grid of coffee photography
- **Shop** — Coming-soon shop section for coffee products
- **Contact** — A simple contact form for questions, feedback, and recipe shares
- **Fully Responsive** — Optimized layouts for both desktop and mobile, including a mobile dropdown menu

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone this repository
2. Set up a local WordPress environment (e.g. LocalWP, XAMPP, or Docker)
3. Import the theme/site files into your WordPress installation
4. Activate the theme and configure widgets/menus as needed
5. Run the site locally or deploy to your hosting provider
